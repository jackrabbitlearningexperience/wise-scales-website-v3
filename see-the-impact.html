Understand

Every year since 2021

A national survey that covers every state

Mexico’s National Health and Nutrition Survey asks the HWISE questions every year, with samples large enough to estimate water insecurity in each state.

Mexico · INSP · Salud Pública de México, 2025 (opens in new tab)

Evaluate

Statewide

A state targets its poverty programs

After a drought emergency, Nuevo León adopted the HWISE Scale. The state government now uses it to make sure poverty reduction reaches the households most in need.

Nuevo León, Mexico · Annals of Global Health, 2025 (opens in new tab)

Advocate

44% vs 1%

A community made its water crisis impossible to ignore

Walgett’s community-led surveys found 44% of residents water insecure, against 1% nationally. It made the national news, and the state government acted.

Walgett, Australia · Community briefing report (opens in new tab)

Measuring water in a crisis

When disaster makes the headlines, the WISE Scales show what it does to people’s water.

Gaza

October 2025With a ceasefire in Gaza, UNICEF begins assessing the damage to water and wastewater infrastructureRead the article (UNICEF, opens in new tab)

The UN WASH Cluster tracks water insecurity in Gaza every month with the HWISE Scale.

Ukraine

16 June 2023Ukraine: 700,000 people affected by water shortages from dam disasterRead the article (UN News, opens in new tab)

HWISE-4 is part of Ukraine’s humanitarian needs assessment.

Afghanistan

September 2025Kabul’s wells run dry, driving children out of class and into water queuesRead the article (Arab News, opens in new tab)

Afghanistan’s needs assessment asks the HWISE questions of several people in each household.

Sri Lanka

November 2025Cyclone ‘Ditwah’ wreaks havoc in Sri Lanka, causing floods and mass displacementRead the article (APP, opens in new tab)

IWMI and IFPRI are measuring what the cyclone did to people’s water.

Tonga

16 January 2022‘Volcanic eruption contaminated entire water supply in Tonga’Read the article (Anadolu Agency, opens in new tab)

IWDA used the IWISE-4 Scale after the eruption, linking water insecurity to mental distress.

Ohio, USA

euronews.17 February 2023US official reassures residents near toxic train derailment that their water and air are safeRead the article (Euronews / AP, opens in new tab)

Researchers used the HWISE Scale to measure what residents actually experienced.

Lebanon

22 December 2014Water Shortages in Lebanon Devastate Syrian RefugeesRead the article (VOA, opens in new tab)

In Beirut, the HWISE Scale showed refugees far more water insecure than their neighbors.

More than a decade of making water insecurity visible

  1. 2013 Annie Otieno describes water insecurity in western Kenya
  2. 2016 An IMMANA research grant funds a globally comparable scale
  3. 2017 HWISE data collection in 28 sites across 23 countries
  4. 2019 The HWISE Scale is published in BMJ Global Health
  5. 2019 Gallup, UNESCO and Northwestern agree to build an individual measure
  6. 2021 Mexico becomes the first country to use WISE in a national survey
  7. 2021 The IWISE Scale is published in BMJ Global Health
  8. 2023 With FAO, across 25 countries: water insecurity goes hand in hand with food insecurity
  9. 2023 WISE Latin America & Caribbean network launches in Mexico City: 60 leaders, 40 institutions, 13 countries
  10. 2023 Nuevo León’s Governor signs a declaration to use the WISE Scales
  11. 2024 WHO/UNICEF JMP recommends the IWISE-4 Scale for SDG 6 monitoring
  12. 2024 charity: water makes HWISE a key performance indicator
  13. 2025 FoodWISE Asia-Pacific network launches in Sydney
  14. 2025 Public Health Champions Medal from Mexico’s National Institute of Public Health
  15. 2026 Africa Water and Food Experiences Network launches in Nairobi on World Africa Day
  16. 2026 HWISE-4 in humanitarian needs assessments in 21+ countries
